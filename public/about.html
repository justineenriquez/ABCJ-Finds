About ABCJ Finds

ABCJ Finds is a responsive restaurant database application powered by MongoDB. Search, filter, add, edit, and delete restaurant information through one clear interface.

View restaurants
▤
Platform information

One organized directory

Each MongoDB record includes the restaurant name, cuisine, Philippine location, rating, and contact number.

Purpose

Why we built it

We built ABCJ Finds to keep restaurant information in one accessible place and make database management straightforward.
